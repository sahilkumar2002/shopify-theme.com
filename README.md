# Indian Rug Store — Luxury Editorial Shopify Theme (Jaipur Rugs Style)

> Museum-quality rugs tied knot by knot across ancestral Indian weaver villages. A modern Online Store 2.0 theme inspired by the editorial aesthetics of Jaipur Rugs.

## ✨ Key Features & Enhancements

- **Premium Editorial Header**:
  - Sticky glassmorphic navigation (transparent over hero, transitions to crisp solid white with subtle shadow on scroll).
  - Centered multi-column mega menu with featured artisan spotlight cards.
  - Slide-down interactive predictive search modal.
  - Order of functional icons: Search, Account, Wishlist (with live heart pop badge), and Sanctuary Bag Cart Drawer.
  - Rotating announcement bar at the top (4-second smooth fade transitions).
- **Interactive Bag & Ajax Cart Drawer**:
  - Slide-in side drawer with live item count, quantity +/- incrementers, subtotal, and free white-glove shipping progress bar.
  - Direct 1-click proceed to custom checkout.
- **Client Wishlist**:
  - Live item counter, heart animation, and drawer/page management for saved pieces.
- **Standalone Editorial Subpages**:
  - **About Us** (`about-us.html` / `page.about.json`): Ancestral heritage, human loom philosophy, and artisan metrics.
  - **Contact Concierge** (`contact-us.html` / `page.contact.json`): Direct design atelier inquiry form, studio details, and FAQs.
  - **Careers & Guild** (`careers.html` / `page.careers.json`): Live interactive job board with discipline filters (Design, Community, Trade Concierge, Editorial, Master Loom), role blueprint drawers, and digital application form.
  - **Brand Story** (`brand-story.html`): The Manchaha spontaneous weaving initiative and artisan biographies.
  - **Bespoke Checkout** (`checkout.html`): 3-step luxury checkout flow with Express Pay (Shop Pay, PayPal, Google Pay), white-glove delivery options, and promo code recalculation.
- **Performance & Animations**:
  - Swiper v11 responsive touch-carousel for bestselling heirloom pieces.
  - Bulletproof AOS scroll animations with dual safety fallback guarantees to prevent blank sections.

## 📦 Shopify Theme Deployment

1. Download or use the pre-packaged zip archive: `indianrugstore-jaipur-luxury-redesign.zip`.
2. Go to **Shopify Admin** → **Online Store** → **Themes**.
3. Click **Add Theme** → **Upload zip file**.
4. Preview and click **Publish**.
