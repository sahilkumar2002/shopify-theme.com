Our Philosophy & Roots

Every Knot Holds a Soulful Story

Around 40 years ago, in the heart of Jaipur — the historic Pink City of palaces and master crafts — Indian Rug Store was born out of an unyielding reverence for heritage pit-loom knotting.

Today, our carpets connect remote artisanal villages directly with discerning homes worldwide. By eliminating intermediaries, we safeguard ancestral weaving genealogies and ensure dignity, honor, and financial independence for thousands of rural women weavers.
