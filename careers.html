The Atelier Culture

Why Build Your Career With Us

We operate at the rare intersection of uncompromising ancestral artistry, conscious social justice, and contemporary architectural elegance.

Ancestral Artistry & Modern Design

Collaborate with legendary master weavers, architectural studios, and textile curators using highland Ghazni wool, botanical indigo, and raw mulberry silk.

Grassroots Human Dignity

We bypass all middlemen, delivering looms, raw wool, and fair living wages directly to rural doorsteps. Every team member directly touches artisan lives.

Unhurried Excellence

We consciously reject fast-fashion rush and burnout cycles. We celebrate deep focus, tactile exploration, and pieces crafted to endure for generations.

Global Sanctuary Footprint

Work from our Mansarovar design studios in Jaipur, international trade outposts, or flexible hybrid home studios with white-glove collaborative support.

Studio Living

Where Ancestral Looms Meet Contemporary Architecture

Our teams do not sit in sterile cubicles. Our Jaipur atelier is bathed in natural desert light, filled with hanging yarn skeins dyed with pomegranate peels and indigo, and resonates with the rhythmic clatter of sample looms.

Whether sketching bespoke CAD renderings for AD100 architects or traveling into the Thar desert to hear an artisan’s spontaneous song translated into knot patterns, working here is an immersive sensory dialogue.

“Working at Indian Rug Store taught me that design without human conscience is empty. Here, every knot we specify directly changes the destiny of a village family.” — Ananya Mathur, Senior Curation Lead

Senior Textile & Rug Designer

Jaipur Atelier / Hybrid • Full-Time • Design & Architecture Guild
Design Guild

Lead bespoke rug commissions for top-tier global architects and interior firms. Translate spatial blueprints into intricate knot-by-knot graph schemes (Naksha), formulate botanical dye palettes, and oversee prototype development alongside village guild masters.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Develop seasonal collections inspired by Rajasthani architectural heritage and modernist minimalism.
  • Work directly with master weavers in Dausa and Sikar to refine textural density and yarn spinning.
  • Collaborate with international trade clients on custom scale, color rendering, and fiber choices.
  • Conduct botanical dye laboratory tests with walnut husks, madder root, and organic indigo.

Desired Qualifications:

Degree in Textile/Surface Design (NID, NIFT, or international equivalent); 4+ years in woven textile or rug design; proficiency in Pointcarré, NedGraphics, Photoshop, and hand graph drafting; deep cultural empathy for rural artisan communities.

Artisan Community & Loom Outreach Manager

Rajasthan Weaver Hubs (Dausa, Bikaner) • Full-Time · Field Based • Community & Sourcing
Grassroots Field

Serve as the crucial empathetic bridge between our Jaipur design sanctuary and 600+ rural weaving villages. Oversee raw wool delivery logistics, conduct regular artisan welfare audits, support women weaver self-help groups, and document genealogy records for every loom creation.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Spend 3-4 days weekly in rural artisan hamlets facilitating weaver community meetings.
  • Coordinate the Manchaha initiative, empowering weavers to design their own spontaneous carpets.
  • Ensure 100% on-time, transparent payments directly to women artisans' individual bank accounts.
  • Organize village health checkup camps and artisan children educational scholarships.

Desired Qualifications:

Fluency in Hindi and Rajasthani dialects; 3+ years experience in rural development, grassroots craft NGOs, or artisanal supply chains; valid driver's license and passion for rural field engagement.

Private Client Concierge & Trade Specialist

New York / London / Hybrid • Full-Time • Client Experience Guild
Client Concierge

Manage high-touch relationships with discerning private collectors, luxury hospitality developers, and interior design firms across North America and Europe. Provide in-home styling consultations, coordinate physical yarn pom swatches, and guide clients from initial loom conception through white-glove delivery.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Act as personal concierge for high-net-worth clients commissioning custom heirloom carpets ($10k - $80k+).
  • Curate tailored digital presentations and tactile material swatches for architectural project pitches.
  • Provide bi-weekly progress photo dispatches directly from the village loom to the client.
  • Represent the atelier at international design salons (Maison&Objet, Salone del Mobile, ICFF).

Desired Qualifications:

5+ years luxury interior design trade or high-end bespoke home furnishings sales; impeccable written and verbal communication; established network with AD100 designers and architectural specifiers.

Visual Storyteller & Editorial Content Lead

Jaipur Atelier / Hybrid • Full-Time • Editorial & Brand Story
Brand Story

Author the visual and poetic identity of Indian Rug Store. Travel with documentary cinematographers to remote desert loom villages, capture the intimate hands and voices of our artisan families, and craft evocative editorial publications, cinematic campaign films, and exhibition catalogs.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Direct biannual editorial lookbooks, campaign photo shoots, and artisan profile documentary shorts.
  • Write lyrical, culturally authentic narrative copy for our global digital platforms, print journals, and museum collaborations.
  • Curate visual storytelling for our physical design sanctuaries and gallery pop-ups worldwide.
  • Collaborate with international architectural and design media editors (Architectural Digest, Elle Decor, Wallpaper*).

Desired Qualifications:

Portfolio demonstrating editorial writing and art direction in luxury, craftsmanship, or cultural documentation; deep aesthetic sensitivity; hands-on creative direction experience.

Master Loom Apprenticeship Coordinator

Mansarovar Atelier, Jaipur • Full-Time · Studio Based • Heritage Preservation
Heritage Guild

Preserve rare and endangered Rajasthani weaving techniques by orchestrating our generational apprentice academy. Mentor aspiring young weavers, document centuries-old knotting notations, and oversee museum restoration commissions for antique Persian and Mughal textile fragments.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Structure curricula and hands-on workshops for 120+ young generational apprentices annually.
  • Work with senior master weavers to record oral folklore and weaving chants passed through memory.
  • Conduct archival studies on museum textile conservation and antique repair methods.
  • Facilitate educational visits for international design universities and textile scholars.

Desired Qualifications:

Extensive background in hand-weaving, textile conservation, or craft pedagogical instruction; master-level understanding of Persian asymmetrical knotting and Indian flatweaves; passion for passing cultural heritage to youth.
