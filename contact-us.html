Frequently Asked Questions

How long does an authentic bespoke hand-knotted rug take to create?

Depending on the knot density (ranging from 120 to 300 knots per square inch) and dimensions, a bespoke hand-knotted rug takes between 10 to 18 weeks. Each piece is hand-spun, botanically dyed, hand-tied knot by knot, and washed with mountain spring water before reaching your home.

How does the 30-Day In-Home Sanctuary Trial work?

We believe a rug must harmonize with your home’s natural morning and evening light. Receive your heirloom and live with it for up to 30 days. If the tone or scale does not feel perfectly aligned with your sanctuary, our white-glove team will arrange a seamless exchange or full refund.

Do you offer complimentary worldwide shipping?

Yes. All acquisitions over $500 include complimentary insured white-glove air courier delivery across North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific with end-to-end provenance tracking directly from our Jaipur atelier.

Can interior designers request private yarn sample boxes?

Our trade concierge provides accredited architects and interior designers with curated swatch sample boxes containing highland wool and pure mulberry silk tufts in over 200 custom botanical dye shades.
